About Urban Nest

Housing is the foundation, but not the full journey.

Founded in May 2024 by Ben Gyles and Silvia Costa, Urban Nest Property Solutions was created to bridge the critical gap between quality housing and specialist care in Croydon and London.

What is supported housing?

Supported housing combines secure, self-contained or shared accommodation with tailored support services to help vulnerable adults, young care leavers, or people recovering from crisis live independently. Unlike standard residential tenancies, the property is leased to a specialist care provider or Registered Provider who delivers day-to-day care and oversees tenancy management.

How does a care provider lease work?

We partner with vetted care providers and Registered Providers who sign long-term corporate leases, typically lasting between 3 and 5+ years. Landlords receive guaranteed rent with zero void periods and no day-to-day tenant management hassles. The care provider takes full operational responsibility for the interior upkeep, resident matching, and compliant property stewardship.

What types of property do you look for?

We look for single-family houses, high-quality HMOs, bungalows, blocks of self-contained flats, studios, and land with residential development potential. We specifically seek properties suitable for social housing, supported living, and C2 children's homes. Properties must offer safe residential surroundings, excellent transport links, and potential for compliant, dignified care adaptation.

Do you buy properties?

Yes. In addition to securing long-term leases for care providers, Urban Nest actively purchases assets outright. We buy residential properties needing refurbishment, portfolios, commercial buildings with residential conversion potential, and land across Croydon, South London, and Surrey. We work with private sellers, landlords, and investment partners seeking practical, ethical exits.

Which areas do you cover?

Our primary focus is Croydon, where we were founded and host our 'Building Croydon Better' community series. We actively cover South London boroughs, wider Greater London (including East London boroughs like Tower Hamlets, Hackney, and Newham), and Surrey. We prioritise locations with established social infrastructure and documented council care commissioning needs.

How do you make sure housing is ethical?

We operate by our principle of 'profit, not profiteering'. We never compromise tenant safety or dignity for yield. Every property undergoes strict physical, environmental, and compliance checks before leasing. We only partner with accredited, ethical providers, verify that accommodation meets real care pathways, and never support inflated or predatory Housing Benefit arrangements.

Do landlords pay you?

In most supported living leasing arrangements, property owners and landlords pay no agency fees or commissions because our costs are structured directly with partner care providers or registered providers. Contact our team for an exact, transparent breakdown of commercial terms for your specific property or portfolio.

Can I list one property or a portfolio?

Both. We work with individual landlords holding a single vacant 2-bed flat or house, as well as institutional owners, developers, and funds managing multi-unit blocks or extensive property portfolios. Every owner receives a complimentary 30-minute portfolio review to identify optimal leasing terms, yield security, and positive social impact.
